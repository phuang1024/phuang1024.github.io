UIUC has a large amount of pianos and practice rooms, spread across multiple
buildings. As someone that enjoys the subtleties of piano tuning, voicing, and
condition, I created a growing catalog of pianos around campus. Plus, exploring
buildings is fun.

The catalog documents piano conditions from a musician's point of view. I
record excerpts on each piano, and describe the piano status based on that
experience.

https://uiucpianocatalog.readthedocs.io
