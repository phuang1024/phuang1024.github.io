The mandelbrot set is beautiful when rendered. Even more beautiful are
related fractals like the buddhabrot and nebulabrot.

I experimented with rendering these fractals, applying creative color schemes,
writing CUDA kernels for the best performance, and making an interactive GUI
to view the render.
